Paste each block into App Store Connect → your app → the version's localization. Limits: name 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100 characters (comma-separated, no spaces).

**Strategy:** the name targets the two best keywords (`GLP-1` + `Shot Tracker`); the subtitle adds `injection`, `log`, `dose`, `reminder`; the keyword field holds everything else. Apple combines words across name, subtitle and keywords, so no word is repeated (e.g. `weight` + `loss` in keywords still ranks for "weight loss tracker").

**Brand names left out on purpose:** Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and Zepbound are trademarks. Using them in the name, subtitle or keywords commonly gets apps rejected (Guideline 2.3.7 / 5.2.1). Generic drug names (semaglutide, tirzepatide) are fine.
